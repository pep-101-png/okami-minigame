# Sunward Unity — integrated restoration review

Target: **Unity 6.6 / 6000.6.6f1**. Work is confined to `unity/` on `sunward-unity-port`. The original Three.js game, GLB, Blender workspaces and existing saves are not overwritten.

## Implemented

- **Original wolf appearance:** the source PNGs were already pixel-correct. The builder forced double-sided rendering on every material, exposing the black reversed ink shells across the white skin. Materials now use the original GLB's one-sided flags, explicit mesh-slot bindings, sRGB 2D maps and Repeat wrapping (the original UVs extend outside 0–1). Rough fur shading is retained without specular highlights. Mesh, UVs, rig and proportions are unchanged. The repaired FBX was rendered with those maps/culling in a separate WebGL diagnostic; this is not a Unity screenshot.
- **Weapons/combat:** the actual original Spear and Sundial meshes remain in use. Both are displayed on a lacquered home wall rack. Mouth equipment and weapon attacks are enabled only in trials; exit, retreat and rest hide carried equipment without changing ownership. Q switches owned weapons inside the arena. Sundial still uses the original **110-coin unlock at Taro**; existing progression is preserved, not silently reset or granted. Original circular flame strokes and weapon motion were restored. Heavy/light buffering now handles changes of attack category, missing animation states produce specific errors, and original light/heavy/charged/air clips are validated for both weapons. Heavy and flame cues are distinct generated placeholders, not newly recorded weapon audio.
- **Sleep:** the source bake contained inverted elbows/knees and unstable roll. Corrected joint planes and forward paw placement were baked only into the Unity copy's three sleep clips. Body breathing, lowering/rising trajectories and all non-sleep actions are retained. Prior clips remain as `Sleep_Down_Legacy`, `Sleep_Loop_Legacy`, `Sleep_Up_Legacy`. The three corrected actions are animated skeletal transitions, not a static pose. Blender numeric checks and rendered FBX poses from both sides were inspected; see SleepInspection.png. Bed contact and Unity transitions still need Play Mode review.
- **Environment placement:** disconnected small rock/grass components are grounded against the actual terrain height data. The numerical run adjusted **192 components**; architecture, bridge fittings, large joined landscape surfaces and intentional submerged rocks are excluded to avoid moving buildings or cliffs. Trunk/rock collider heights now follow terrain too. This is a targeted placement pass, not a claim every object was manually repositioned.
- **River:** movement out of existing deep water is allowed instead of bisecting between two underwater points. After brief submersion the player is moved to a clear nearby bank, with last-safe/home fallback, reset movement state and camera recovery. The pause menu also offers a safe return home in town. Swimming has not been added.
- **NPCs:** all seven spawns are projected onto safe walkable ground with footprint clearance. Short local strolls use swept checks against the original building/rail/rock boundaries, resident separation, cliff/water rejection and trapped-resident recovery. Taro's interaction follows his current position. This uses analytic navigation rather than a new NavMesh dependency. Source NPC visuals are static meshes; they do not gain a new skeletal walking animation in this update.
- **Rendering:** procedural wood/stone wash relief, softer view-edge pigment, richer ambient response, downstream water with depth-based shore fade, opaque-scene refraction and environment highlights; URP depth/opaque textures, 4× MSAA, SMAA, four shadow cascades, 4096 shadow map, wider shadow range; ACES, restrained bloom, warm grading, vignette, procedural sky, drifting painted petals, and a downsampled URP SSAO renderer feature. Lightweight settings remain available. The SSAO package feature is configured through its serialized settings because its settings class is internal; absence is reported rather than silently claimed as enabled.
- **Import reliability:** original PNGs are forced to 2D before import, FBX automatic legacy material conversion stays disabled, generated main-asset names match filenames, existing Animator/mesh catalogue GUIDs are retained on rebuild, and validation checks texture dimensions, finite geometry, original culling, weapon meshes and every attack reference. Genuine errors are not suppressed.

Unity Editor is unavailable in the cloud. The graphics target is therefore **implemented for review, not verified as a finished high-fidelity game**.

## Install and test locally

1. Close Unity and keep a copy of the current project. Extract the update ZIP and copy its **contents** into the existing project folder containing Assets, Packages and ProjectSettings. Replace the included files. Do not delete Library or save files as a routine step.
2. Reopen with 6000.6.6f1 and let import/compilation finish. Use **Sunward > Create or Rebuild V3 Scene** once. It rebuilds generated scene/prefab/controller content; keep any manually edited scene separately first. The scene is **Assets/Sunward/Scenes/Windflower.unity**.
3. Run **Sunward > Validate Imported Project**, and optionally **Sunward > Validate Character Deformations** outside Play Mode. The latter samples/bakes the actual imported skins to catch non-finite/exploded meshes; it cannot certify attractive motion or catch every intersection.
4. Press Play and enter Windflower. Check white fur/red markings through Idle/Walk/Jog/Sprint; inspect wall weapons, NPC clearance and grounded rocks. Sleep with E near the home bed and wake with movement/E. Check paws/ankle fur against the bed from both sides.
5. Unlock Sundial at Taro if not already owned, enter the arena, switch with Q, and test tap LMB/J (light), tap RMB/L (heavy), both holds (charged), air attack, dodge and exit. Carried weapons and attack controls should stop on leaving the trial. The home rack should return.
6. Test repeated river recovery, both crossings, camera control after recovery, pause return-home, saving/loading and both quality presets. Clear the Console before each reproduction and report the first newly generated red error if import or play fails.

## Remaining artwork and quality limits

Original wolf maps are **128–512 pixels**, preserved exactly. Existing town maps/geometry remain the source artwork; procedural detail and lighting do not turn these into 4K assets. A larger art upgrade still needs authored higher-resolution building/roof/stone/terrain sets with matching normals/roughness, richer vegetation variants/LODs, and animated NPC assets. Final shadow/AO/colour/water tuning and a performance budget require viewing and profiling the complete scene in Unity on the target PC. Sleep bed contact, attack readability and bank/collider edge cases also require local Play Mode verification.

Rollback: the prior Unity version is available at commit `bc7306c`; legacy sleep actions are embedded in the new FBX. The original Three.js and approved Blender sources remain untouched.
