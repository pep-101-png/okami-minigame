# Sunward — divine wolf build

Extract the ZIP into a NEW folder and double-click START-GAME.cmd. Open the Local address it prints. Keep the terminal open. Node.js is required; the launcher also finds portable Node folders on your Desktop.

This is the playable 3D Sunward game with the uploaded wolf derivative, softened neck/shoulder fur, animated ankle and shoulder fur, lowered idle tail and lifted running tail, and faster full-body light/heavy/charged/aerial action poses. The town, NPCs, enemies, sound and activities remain playable.

WASD moves; Shift runs; Space jumps; left click chains light attacks (hold to charge); right click performs heavy attacks (hold to charge); B barks; V growls; M mutes. See the game’s controls for dodge and other actions.

Earlier versions remain on their original Git branches. Inkbound is separate and has not been modified. The previous Sunward wolf asset and adapter are retained. This release is a reviewed iteration, not a claim of final artistic perfection.
