AMATERASU — IDLE / WALK / JOG / SPRINT

Open Amaterasu-Movement.blend in Blender 4.3 or newer.
All six original textures are packed. No add-ons or texture folders are required.

PLAY
The rig is selected in Pose Mode and Sprint is already active.
Hover over the 3D view and press Space to play/pause.
Use the Action-name dropdown in the lower Action Editor to choose:
  Idle   — original breathing/idle, unchanged; 240 frames / 4 seconds.
  Walk   — original slow walk, unchanged; 72 frames / 1.2 seconds.
  Jog    — approved RUN_REFINED, renamed only; 40 frames / 0.667 seconds.
  Sprint — new long-stride gallop; 32 frames / 0.533 seconds.
Legacy_Run is the older pre-refinement Run, kept only for rollback.

The shared 1–1440 playback range repeats complete cycles of every Action.
Do not change the playback range when switching Actions.
The closing key (cycle length + 1) repeats the first key; Cycles modifiers
repeat the curves with matching boundary tangents.
Hover over the Action Editor and press Home to fit the selected Action's keys.

VIEW
Z > Material Preview shows the original appearance.
Turn Viewport Overlays off for a clean motion preview, on again to pose controls.
Numpad 3: side. Numpad 1: front. Ctrl+Numpad 1: rear.
Without a numpad, use View > Viewpoint. Middle mouse orbits; wheel zooms.

These are IN-PLACE cycles. Forward game travel is not baked into the root.
Corresponding source-unit travel speeds: Walk 1.667, Jog 7.2, Sprint 14.625.
Sprint travels 7.8 source units per cycle, versus Jog's 4.8; it also has
new extended/gathered poses and different footfall/contact timing.
The old rig checkpoint and approved Jog project remain separate, unchanged files.
