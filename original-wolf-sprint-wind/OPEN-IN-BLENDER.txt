AMATERASU — SPRINT POLISH + WIND

Blender 4.3 or newer. Open Amaterasu-Movement.blend, then press Space.
No add-ons or permission to run Python scripts are required.

The selected wolf Action is Sprint_Polished (32 frames at 60 fps).
Select WOLF_RIG in the Outliner. In the lower Action Editor, use the Action
name dropdown to choose Idle, Walk, Jog, Sprint or Sprint_Polished.
Sprint is the previous accepted gallop, preserved for rollback and comparison.
Sprint_Polished preserves its body, shoulders, hips, paws, toes and leg curves.
Only head, neck and existing tail-control animation has been revised.

Wind appears on Sprint_Polished only. It is an independent collection:
05 · Sprint wind — separate effect. The wolf has no wind modifiers or materials.
For proper wind shading, use Material Preview or Rendered viewport shading
(the third or fourth sphere icon at the upper right of the 3D viewport).

WIND SPEED AND FADE
In collection 05 select SPRINT_WIND · speed control, then open Object Properties
> Custom Properties. 'speed' 0–20 controls the wind only; 14.625 is full sprint.
'strength' 0–1 changes its overall strength. Neither control retimes the wolf.
Keyframe speed to fade the wind as a future sprint controller accelerates or
slows down. The smoothstep response avoids a hard on/off threshold.

Optional fade preview: select that control empty, then select Wind_Speed_Demo
in the Action Editor. Play frames 1–129: accelerate, hold, decelerate. The wolf
continues its unchanged 32-frame gallop. To return to steady wind, unlink the
empty's Action with X, then set speed to 14.625. Keep Sprint_Polished on WOLF_RIG.

EXPORT NOTES
Bake/sample the wolf's existing constrained rig onto deform bones at 60 fps;
export one Sprint_Polished cycle, frames 1–33 (frame 33 duplicates frame 1).
The wind's Blender drivers, attribute shader and shape-key driver animation
are not a directly portable glTF particle effect. Recreate it in Three.js with
pooled curved ribbon strips, tapered widths, pigment noise and a speed-driven
opacity/length uniform. Smooth the actual movement speed in the game controller.
The wind collection is isolated so it can be excluded from the character export.
No Three.js/game/viewer changes are included in this project.
