AMATERASU — FLUID SPRINT / PLAYBACK REPAIR

Open Amaterasu-Movement.blend in Blender 4.3 or newer. Press Space.
Sprint_Fluid is selected on WOLF_RIG in the lower Action Editor.
The previous Sprint_Polished and Sprint are retained, along with Idle, Walk,
Jog and Legacy_Run. None of their existing animation curves were edited.

VIEWPORT QUALITY
At the top-right of Blender, the View Layer dropdown offers:
FULL · Painted wind — the finished painted wind effect.
FAST · Lightweight wind — three opaque streaks without alpha/noise shading.
Both use the same textured wolf and animation; the fast streaks do not render.
The finished effect has not been deleted or replaced.

The scene remains 60 fps, FPS Base 1.0, with Frame Dropping enabled.
This keeps real-time speed if your hardware cannot draw every frame, but
cannot create missing GPU performance. For judging smoothness, enable
Viewport Overlays > Text Info to see playback FPS. Maximise the viewport
with Ctrl+Space if desired. Material Preview preserves the original look.
Solid shading is a further optional motion-checking mode; return to Material
Preview to see the original materials. No Python auto-run or add-ons required.

WHAT WAS FIXED
The CPU bottleneck was two live SIMPLE subdivision modifiers used for joint
bend resolution. Their already-visible surface is now precomputed before
skinning, rather than recalculated every frame. This is not reduced detail:
retained-pose evaluated vertices and normals match exactly. Original base
mesh datablocks remain stored as ORIGINAL_BASE · Object_2 / Object_6, and the
previous complete project is preserved separately for rollback.

The neck controls and weights were working. The old animation moved them
mostly together and explicitly locked the head to a world-space target.
Sprint_Fluid introduces phased lower/upper-neck counter-flexion and partial
rather than absolute gaze stabilisation. No bones, constraints or weights
were rebuilt. Body, legs, tail, stride and gallop timing retain their curves.

EXPORT
Sprint_Fluid is an in-place 32-frame loop at 60 fps. Export/sample frames
1–33, with 33 duplicating 1. Bake the existing IK constraints onto deform
bones for game export. Exclude preview objects and the independent wind
collection from the character export. Wind drivers/shaders need recreating
as ribbon effects in Three.js. No game or browser-viewer files were changed.
