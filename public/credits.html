Sunward · asset credits

The playable divine wolf is a painted and animated derivative of Amaterasu the Japanese Fox God by theamazingdonovan207, licensed under CC BY 4.0. Changes include neck/shoulder geometry, tail, ivory shading, vermilion paint, rig, fur deformation and animation. Attribution does not imply endorsement.

The earlier wolf asset is retained in the project and uses the Khronos Fox asset: PixelMannen geometry (CC0), tomkranis rig/animation and AsoboStudio/scurest conversion (CC BY 4.0). Town scenery, enemies and synthesized audio are project content. No copyrighted Ōkami music is included.

Canine vocal recordings

Wolf growls, hurt cries and howl cue: Lari Nieminen (zookeeper), Battle for Wesnoth, GNU GPL v2 or later. Original Ogg recordings are included; playback gain/rate varies. Audio license. The howl cue uses wolf-die-3.ogg, a wolf cry from the pack.
