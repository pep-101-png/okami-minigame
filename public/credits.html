Sunward · asset credits

This wolf review uses a new connected anatomical coat, rebound to the rig and original locomotion of the Fox asset in Khronos glTF Sample Assets, with added shoulder/mane controls, fresh UVs, ivory paint, red markings, an optional spear, and added/retimed action clips. It is an original wolf interpretation based on a fox source, not a model extracted from Ōkami.
