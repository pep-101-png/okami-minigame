# Village life, weapon mastery and moonlit exploration

Four linked activities use recovered original Ōkami models, paint, motion banks and existing recovered audio. The new courtyard, fishing rules, restoration projects and secret route are adapted Sunward gameplay, not recovered original game scripts.

- **Weapon mastery:** the notice/scroll on the dojo's eastern side starts three stages for each of the fifteen instruments (45 lessons). Learn practices six hits, two charged contacts and a brush hit; Apply/Master require the awakened instrument and its real ability. Master also requires brush contacts and at most five damage contacts. Exorcism requires actual projectile deflections. Objectives count actual freezes, burn applications, charged pulses, resource recovery, successive cuts and falling-sword hits. The separate practice courtyard restores the prior position, equipped weapon and vitals on completion, cancellation or defeat. First clears award 150/300/600 coins and 100/200/400 XP; repeats and practice kills cannot farm normal coins/XP. The existing awakening objectives remain in place. U is disabled in practice.
- **Living pond:** visit the north bank of Ammy's pond, talk to the angler, and offer produce. Original fish use their inspected native `_003` swimming loops; `_002` includes a leap and is deliberately not looped. Hold R and draw from Ammy to a visible fish. Hold attack to reel, release to cool tension; constant pulling breaks the line. Six species have day/night/feeding/restoration availability. Lifetime catches and kept fish are saved separately. Sell kept fish to the angler or donate them to restoration. The native swim loop runs faster while hooked; this is an adapted fishing presentation.
- **Village restoration:** the noticeboard accepts coins, produce and fish for a riverside tea garden with seated original visitors, an open pig-yard shelter with bedding/bowl, first-bridge flowers/lanterns, and a pond retreat with rare night char. Buildings use original props. Each reveal is skippable; contributions are permanent and charged once. Praise records help and is not spent. Daytime pilgrims use the new seats and native tea gestures.
- **Moonlit trail:** hints come through the journal and the elder's tea conversations. Examine the flowers beyond the first bridge after dark, then walk through the revealed passage. A separate planted island has two imp encounters and Bloom, Power Slash and Cherry Bomb seals. Saved progress persists between visits. Both encounters and all seals are required for the one-time chest/cutscene: 400 coins, 500 XP and the Moon Charm (+3 Ink/sec outside combat). The southern gate uses swept crossing to return to the village, including a fast step across the gate plane.

## Related awakening corrections

- Seven Strike now schedules seven actual cutting contacts, not a single replacement pulse.
- Exorcism's weakening increases received damage for a short window; brush slashes also clear original imp cannon shells, as well as the existing ink bolts.
- Awakened rank scales special damage and supported healing/Ink recovery by 10% per rank, alongside the existing weapon damage/reach ranks.
- Awakened barks retain the approved weapon colour/spin and add their element's gameplay effect (burn, lightning damage, freeze, wind push, weakening or capped recovery).
- Guardian victories record the existing awakening boss objective before leaving combat. The earned second/third form requirements, P testing shortcut, approved ultimate and Eighth Wonder impact timings/audio are preserved.
