# Heaven and boar paddock repair

Desktop rollback/base: 1ad16ca739d2a1f2ac3be3faabdedd9e8e00912d. Existing Windflower runtime components and original asset layout are updated; no separate demo scene.

- Boar and piglet retain their known working spawn coordinates and gain a native timber paddock at X15.5–23/Z27.8–36.5. Their routes stay within it, including the actual packed body radii (0.829m and 0.553m) plus clearance. Bucket and original straw are inside the yard. The thirteen-animal population is preserved.
- The original meadow and terrace contain zero-alpha vertices intended as stage fades. The former ground shader multiplied that fade into alpha clipping, creating visible holes despite complete collision geometry. Ground now remains opaque; native stone faces retain back-face culling to avoid rendering their inverted ink shells. A broad non-colliding cloud underlay covers the backdrop beneath the garden.
- Ground uses world-space blends of existing original meadow and cloud paintings, with a pearl/mint/lilac lighting palette. This avoids stretching a bland texture across the entire island and aligns the paint across source terrain patches. Original texture pixels are unchanged; no generated painting is shipped.
- The full bridge beneath the keeper is replaced with a solid original carved-stone dais. Its base is slightly sunk into the lowest sampled supporting terrain. The keeper remains on her original idle animation. The close-up diagnostic includes the actual original skin and idle pose, with feet on the dais.
- Shared render visibility lives in NativeInteriorDressing, the existing late-running visibility owner. It hides other world renderers during heaven visits while retaining garden/player descendants and world-space player effects whose bounds are in the realm. The original room rules and prior render flags still apply outside heaven. A separate competing visibility controller was explicitly avoided because its settings would be overwritten in LateUpdate.
- A keeper-owned conversation guard consumes the finishing interaction before another conversation can open. Final dialogue buttons say E · Finish. The player can finish with E and speak again on a later press; Escape is not required.
- Original standing side gates, ceremonial paper decorations, gongs and lit candles dress the approach and keeper area. Approved weapon artwork and the user-approved layered bark/audio bindings are unchanged.

Validation: selected production runtime scripts compile with engine doubles, zero errors. Fifteen actual interaction/visibility checks cover opening, paging, finishing and reopening keeper dialogue, existing late-update visibility rules, return/revisit/disable/enable, original hidden flags, and pooled effects moving into heaven. The broader selected suite also passes (13 festival, 23 village, 38 garden/race, 336 text layout, 915 boss/director checks). 1,353 native/layout/source checks and 57 focused opaque-ground/support/paddock/protected-asset checks pass, along with existing farm/audio and doorway checks. All 131 asset C# syntax trees and introduced metadata are checked before packaging.

Four offline Blender diagnostic views use the runtime JSON, original source geometry/paint and a static original keeper pose. These do not run Unity; particle snapshots and lighting are approximate. Actual Unity shader compilation, gameplay, camera rendering, movement and sound are not verified here. The rendered garden alone does not prove village isolation; that behaviour is checked through the shared production visibility controller with engine doubles.

Only changed Assets/.meta files are included in the downloadable ZIP. Tools, these notes, diagnostics and reports are excluded. The source PR remains stacked on the preserved Desktop branch rather than replacing the obsolete integration project.
