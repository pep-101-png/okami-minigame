## Definition of completion

A saved Blender file or generated image alone is insufficient. Check three angles, source artwork provenance, silhouette/geometry/painting quality against the approved R2 houses, packed image reopening, file integrity and protected-file hashes. Unity performance, animations, collisions and final in-game appearance are not tested by these prototypes.
