AMATERASU — MILESTONE 2: JUMPING AND DODGING
Blender 4.3 or newer. Textures packed. No add-ons or Python trust required.

OPEN AND PLAY
The file opens in scene "03 · Jump and dodge showcase". Press Space.
On-screen titles and timeline markers identify each test. The demonstration
includes gait lead-ins/recoveries, standing/walk/jog/sprint jumps, midair
rebound, falling, soft/heavy landings and all seven evasive Actions.
The previous "02 · Movement showcase" is retained unchanged.

INDIVIDUAL ACTIONS
Choose scene "01 · Action inspection" (Scene dropdown at the top-right).
Select WOLF_RIG. Use the lower Action Editor's Action dropdown:

Jump_Standing / Jump_Walk / Jump_Jog / Jump_Sprint
Jump_Double — airborne rebound with asymmetric tuck, twist and a second arc.
Air_Fall — looping airborne descent pose; no world descent baked into this loop.
Land_Soft / Land_Heavy
Dodge_Left / Dodge_Right / Dodge_Back / Dodge_Forward
Dodge_Walk / Dodge_Jog / Dodge_Sprint

The existing Idle, Walk, Jog, Sprint_Fluid, natural variants, standing and moving
turns, additive look-around and earlier rollback Actions are retained.
The inspection timeline spans 180 frames; use an Action's displayed range to
inspect its one-shot. Air_Fall alone is cyclic. Reset to frame 1 to replay.
Middle-mouse orbit exits camera view; inspect joints from both sides and rear.

PHASES / TRANSITIONS
Pose markers identify anticipation, takeoff, apex, front/hind landing and
recovery. Jump_Double marks ENERGY_GATHER and MAGIC_IMPULSE. Dodges mark loading,
push, airborne, touchdown and return to gait. Custom Action properties record
entry/exit gait and phase where applicable. These phases support later blending.
Standing and moving jumps include recovery into their corresponding gait.
Land_Soft and Land_Heavy recover toward Idle and may be blended into locomotion.
The stage uses native NLA with six-frame action crossfades and longer gait
crossfades, separate continuous root travel, and additive paw-contact correction.

ROOT MOTION / FUTURE EXPORT
New jump and dodge one-shots include root translation. Air_Fall is in place.
The showcase overrides the root with M2_ROOT and adds M2_CONTACT; the library
Actions remain independently selectable. Its linked wolf shares original
mesh/armature data and is not another geometry copy.
Do not export M2 stage, text, cameras or wind objects as part of the character.
Bake existing IK/deform constraints for glTF. Extract root motion or provide an
in-place equivalent in the later game controller; do not apply both world/root
translation twice. Metadata uses original source units, not calibrated metres.
Changing the showcase timing requires rebaking M2_CONTACT with demo.py.
This is authored animation, not a live terrain-adapting controller.

PRESERVATION / PERFORMANCE
Original geometry, UVs, normals, rig, weights, materials and six packed textures
are preserved. Previous Action curves are not rewritten. Original movement
checkpoint remains on the separate amaterasu-movement-system branch.
Walk has no wind; Jog is light; Sprint retains full wind. Existing FAST and FULL
View Layers in the inspection scene remain available. New Actions use native
interpolated curves; no frame handlers or mesh modifiers were added.
60 FPS is the scene timebase. Actual viewport FPS depends on hardware/shading.
No game or browser-viewer files are modified.
